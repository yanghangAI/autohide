**Idea Name:** Multi-scale and structure-aware spatial masking for content-adaptive embedding

**Approach:** Deepen the proven Sobel-JND spatial-masking lever from `idea003/design003` by replacing the single-scale Sobel gradient mask with a multi-scale gradient mask family, and by adding an explicit edge-preserving total-variation penalty on the host residual that concentrates in flat regions — sharpening the encoder's map of where residuals are perceptually invisible vs. where they show.

**Expected Designs:** 3

**Suggested Parent:** runs/idea003/design003

**Baseline Source:** runs/idea003/design003 (current best `Done` entry — score=28.16, PSNR=28.48, ssim=0.945, bit_acc=0.9943, bit_acc_clean_min=0.9949). Builds on its `code/baseline/losses.py` and `code/baseline/exp0_inn_train.py` so the gain from the *mask-quality* axis is measured against the strongest single-design baseline currently available.

**Relationship to prior work:** Extends the spatial-masking axis from `idea003` (current best `Done`, score=28.16) along the *mask-quality* dimension. Distinct from the parallel iter3 idea (`runs/idea006`), which stacks EMA shadow weights on top of `idea003/design003` at the optimizer-side without changing the mask. This idea changes the mask itself and leaves the optimizer untouched, so it is independently composable with EMA in a future iteration. Distinct from `idea001` (frequency/chroma reweighting), `idea002` (attack curriculum), `idea004` (optimizer dynamics), and `idea005` (bit-carrier basis).

**Motivation from the data and code:**

1. **Single-scale Sobel is the obvious bottleneck of the current winner.** `idea003/design003` (28.16, +0.89 over baseline) uses a Gaussian-blurred Sobel-magnitude map at one fixed scale to weight both the YUV term and a new masked-L1 term. A single Sobel kernel detects structure at one frequency band: a tree-canopy texture, a fine wood grain, and a large architectural edge are all "high gradient" but have very different perceptual masking profiles. Multi-scale aggregation (Sobel on luma after Gaussian blurs at σ ∈ {1, 2, 4} px, max-pooled per pixel) captures both fine textures (high-frequency masking) and broad edges (mid-frequency masking) while keeping true flat regions low across all scales — widening the *useful* dynamic range of the mask.

2. **Within idea003, mask family already mattered.** `design002` (variance-pooled, single 7×7 window) scored 27.55; `design001` (Sobel) scored 27.85; `design003` (Sobel + masked L1) scored 28.16. Just changing the mask cue from variance to Sobel was worth +0.30 score at otherwise equal settings. This says the encoder responds to *structural* cues, not just energy cues, and the (mask kind × mask scale) grid still has untested points — multi-scale gradient pooling is the natural one.

3. **An edge-preserving TV term targets the *complement* of the mask.** The Sobel-JND mask down-weights penalty in textured regions; equivalently, it concentrates penalty in flat regions. A complementary TV-style penalty `tv_w * mean((1 − w_norm) · |∇(stego − cover)|)` *explicitly* forbids residual gradient in flat regions — going beyond down-weighting to actively prohibit residual structure where the cover has none. This is a classic steganographic prior (residuals must lie in the host's structural support) and `idea003` already proved cover-derived priors are safe.

4. **Score sensitivity remains PSNR-dominated, with a small bit_acc cost budget.** Best-so-far `idea003/design003` reached PSNR 28.48 with bit_acc 0.9943 (clean_min 0.9949) — i.e. it has already begun trading a small amount of bit_acc for substantial PSNR. Headroom remains on the PSNR side as long as the bit_acc tail does not fall materially below ~0.99. A sharper mask should *reduce* the bit_acc cost per unit PSNR gain, because the encoder gets a more accurate map of where to put residuals (less bleeding of bit-energy into flat regions per bit embedded).

5. **Failure mode is bounded by the parent's safety profile.** The parent demonstrates the YUV+FFL+masked-L1 stack trains stably. Refining the mask shape or adding a small TV penalty is a softer change than (e.g.) adding a deep perceptual term: a poorly-tuned multi-scale mask degrades to a single-scale mask, not to a collapse mode. The LPIPS-style training collapses (`idea001/d001`=2.72) cannot be reproduced by mask-shape changes alone.

**Suggested design directions (Designer to refine):**

1. **Multi-scale Sobel mask (mask refinement only).** Replace the single-scale Sobel + Gaussian-blur in the parent with a max-aggregated multi-scale Sobel: compute Sobel-magnitude on the luma channel after Gaussian blurs at σ ∈ {1.0, 2.0, 4.0} px (three blurred copies of the cover), normalize each per-image to [0, 1] (e.g. divide by 99th percentile to suppress outliers), take per-pixel max across scales to form `M_multi`, then form `w = 1 / (1 + β · M_multi)` with the same β=4.0 as the parent. Use `w` everywhere the parent used its single-scale `w` (both the YUV term and the masked-L1 term). All other parent settings preserved. Tests whether broadening the scale range alone lifts PSNR at fixed bit_acc.

2. **Single-scale Sobel + complementary TV penalty on residual.** Keep the parent's single-scale Sobel mask and its masked-YUV + masked-L1 terms unchanged; *add* a small edge-aware TV term `tv_w * mean((1 − w_norm) · |∇(stego − cover)|)`, where `w_norm = w / max(w)` per image, `∇` is a Sobel gradient on the luma residual, and `tv_w` is small (Designer to pick in [0.2, 0.5]). The `(1 − w_norm)` factor concentrates the TV penalty in flat regions so the term explicitly forbids residual gradient where the cover has none. Tests whether the complementary structural prior adds headroom on top of the parent's stack.

3. **Multi-scale mask + complementary TV penalty (combined).** Stack 1 and 2: use the multi-scale Sobel mask `w_multi` everywhere (YUV term, masked-L1 term, and the complementary TV term's `w_norm`). If the two interventions act on independent failure modes (mask precision vs explicit flat-region prohibition) they should add; if they overlap, this design quantifies the interaction so iter-4 can pick the cheaper of the two.

**Constraints:**
- Parent is `runs/idea003/design003` (Done, score=28.16, current best). Designs build on its `code/baseline/losses.py` and `code/baseline/exp0_inn_train.py` directly. Designs may modify only `baseline/exp0_inn_train.py`, `baseline/inn_model.py`, `baseline/losses.py` (and per-design `code/baseline/` mirrors); must not modify any `infra/` file or change locked hyperparameters (`--batch_size 16`, `--secret_len 896`, `--resolution 256`, `--max_steps 30000`, `--total_steps 30000`).
- All masks must be computed from `cover` only and detached from the autograd graph (`torch.no_grad()`), consistent with `idea003`'s constraint — masks are weights, not gradient sources.
- Each scale's Sobel-magnitude map must be normalized per-image (divide by 99th percentile or max) *before* multi-scale aggregation, so β has consistent semantics across covers and across scales.
- Hard condition `bit_acc_clean = 1.000` at every eval resolution must hold. The parent currently sits at clean bit_acc ≈ 0.998 (`clean_min=0.9949`) — already slightly below 1.0. Designer should keep `secret_weight=20.0`, start with conservative β (≤ 4.0) and `tv_w` (≤ 0.5), and reduce the new term's weight if a design pushes clean bit_acc materially below the parent (e.g. `clean_min` < 0.99).
- LPIPS must remain off (`lpips_w=0.0`) per `idea001` / `idea003`'s collapse findings. EMA, STE clipping, HEM bank, severity annealing all out of scope (each is either failed or being tested on a different axis).
- Inherit the parent's INN settings (`--num_blocks=16`, `--subnet_type=rdb`, `--lr=2e-4`, `--img_weight=1.0`, `--bit_weight=20.0`, `--stage_a_steps=5000`, `--stage_b_steps=10000`) verbatim — this idea isolates the mask-quality axis only.
- The complementary TV term in designs 2/3 must operate on the *residual* `(stego − cover)`, not on `stego` directly — penalizing `|∇stego|` would just blur the cover; penalizing `|∇(stego − cover)|` blurs only the *added* signal in flat regions, which is the intended behavior. Compute the residual gradient with the same Sobel kernel used for the mask, on luma only, to keep the cost negligible.
- Walltime: three Gaussian blurs + three Sobel passes on cover at 256² is <1 ms/step on a 2080Ti; the residual TV term is one extra Sobel on the residual luma, also negligible. All three designs fit comfortably; the parent ran within walltime headroom.
- The Sobel + Gaussian-blur primitives should be reused from the parent's existing helper (no new dependencies); the multi-scale stack is just three calls to that helper at different σ followed by an elementwise max.
