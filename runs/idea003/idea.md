**Idea Name:** Spatial JND masking of host-reconstruction loss for content-adaptive embedding

**Approach:** Reweight the per-pixel host-reconstruction residual by a content-adaptive Just-Noticeable-Difference (JND) mask derived directly from the cover image — local activity / edge / texture energy — so the encoder is encouraged to push residuals into regions where they are perceptually invisible (textured / high-gradient) and away from smooth regions where the eye sees them, raising measured PSNR (and perceptual quality) at fixed bit_acc.

**Expected Designs:** 3

**Suggested Parent:** runs/idea001/design003

**Baseline Source:** runs/idea001/design003 (best `Done` entry to date — score=27.73, PSNR=27.77, bit_acc=0.9993, bit_acc_clean≈1.0; YUV+FFL host loss with no LPIPS).

**Relationship to prior work:** New axis — *spatial / content-adaptive* reweighting of the host-reconstruction loss, complementary to idea001 (frequency- and chroma-band reweighting via YUV+LPIPS+FFL) and idea002 (attack-distribution reshaping). Idea001 redistributes residual energy across *frequency/chroma channels*; this idea redistributes it across *spatial locations within each image*, conditioned on each cover's own content. The two are orthogonal and compose: design003's YUV+FFL term is what makes the per-pixel weighting safe from chroma/frequency collapse. This is also distinct from any "INN architecture / message coding / training schedule" axis, sitting on the cover-side preprocessing / loss-side weighting axis.

**Motivation from the data:**
- `score` is essentially linear in PSNR at the current operating point (saturated bit_acc≈0.999); the dominant lever is reducing perceptually-unnecessary residual energy without lowering bit_acc.
- design003 already saturates the *frequency/chroma* axis of perceptual reshaping and lifts PSNR by +0.44 dB over baseline (+0.46 score). The remaining gap to a much-higher PSNR ceiling is plausibly *spatial*: a uniform-per-pixel L1/YUV/FFL penalty still treats a smooth sky region and a tree-canopy region with equal weight, even though the encoder could safely hide ~10× more residual energy in the canopy region with the same perceptual cost (classic JND / spread-spectrum-in-textured-regions intuition from steganography literature).
- design001/002's collapses (2.72, 16.36) showed perceptual reshaping is high-variance — a *strong, broad* prior (LPIPS) over-penalizes high-frequency residuals everywhere and starves the secret pathway. A spatial JND mask is a *softer* prior: it does not change which frequencies are penalized, only which *locations* are penalized; combined with design003's safe YUV+FFL base, it should not trigger the LPIPS-style collapse mode.
- Design003 still penalizes residuals in flat regions (where the eye sees them) with the same weight as in textured regions (where the eye does not). A 10–30% reduction in the flat-region penalty should free the encoder to move residual *out* of textured regions back into flat regions only if forced to — i.e. push the JND budget toward textured regions, raising PSNR at fixed bit_acc.

**Suggested design directions (Designer to refine):**
1. **Sobel-gradient JND mask on the YUV term.** In design003's `ImageSecretLoss`, replace the unweighted `F.mse_loss(rgb_to_yuv(stego), rgb_to_yuv(cover))` with a per-pixel weighted MSE: `mean(w(cover) * (yuv(stego) - yuv(cover))^2)`, where `w(cover) = 1 / (1 + β · M(cover))` and `M(cover)` is the Gaussian-blurred Sobel gradient magnitude of the cover (luma channel), normalized to [0, 1] per image. β is a hyperparameter (e.g. 4.0) controlling the strength of the down-weighting in textured regions. Mask is computed on cover only (no extra grad path through cover→mask), under `torch.no_grad()`. FFL term left unchanged (it is a global frequency statistic and content-adaptive spatial weighting is ill-defined there).
2. **Variance-pooled JND mask.** Same idea but `M(cover)` is the local std-dev of luma over a 7×7 window (computed via a single conv with a uniform kernel and a square-then-conv trick), capturing texture energy more smoothly than Sobel. Tests whether *texture* (variance) or *edges* (gradient) is the better mask — these are correlated but not identical (e.g. a soft gradient sky has low variance and low edge energy; uniform-noise grass has high variance and moderate edge energy).
3. **Joint mask on YUV + per-pixel FFL surrogate.** Sobel-gradient mask applied to the YUV term *and* to a complementary per-pixel L1 residual term `mean(w(cover) * |stego - cover|)` added with small weight (≈0.5), giving the network a spatial-aware pixel-domain penalty alongside the frequency-domain FFL term. Tests whether adding an explicit spatial-domain term — currently absent from design003 since FFL is purely spectral — provides additional headroom when masked.

**Constraints:**
- Parent is `runs/idea001/design003` (Done, score=27.73). Designs build on its `losses.py` and `exp0_inn_train.py`. Designs may modify only `baseline/exp0_inn_train.py`, `baseline/inn_model.py`, `baseline/losses.py` (and the per-design copies under `code/baseline/`); must not modify any `infra/` file or change locked hyperparameters (`--batch_size 16`, `--secret_len 896`, `--resolution 256`, `--max_steps 30000`, `--total_steps 30000`).
- The JND mask must be computed from `cover` only and detached from the autograd graph (it is a *weight*, not a gradient source); otherwise the encoder can game the mask by deforming the cover-derived weighting field, which is undefined behavior here.
- Mask must be normalized per-image (e.g. divide by max or 99th percentile) so β has consistent semantics across covers; otherwise low-content covers would have ~uniform weighting and high-content covers would be near-zero everywhere.
- Hard condition `bit_acc_clean = 1.000` at every eval resolution must hold. Under-weighting flat regions could in principle let the encoder dump enough energy into textured regions to break extraction in flat regions; Designer should keep `secret_weight=20.0` and start with conservative β (≤ 4.0) to preserve the secret pathway.
- LPIPS must remain off (`lpips_w=0.0`) per design003's finding that LPIPS-driven configurations collapse; this idea is explicitly a spatial-axis extension of design003, not a recombination with LPIPS.
- Designer should confirm the mask computation adds negligible cost (Sobel + Gaussian blur on cover at 256² ≈ <1 ms/step on 2080Ti; well within walltime headroom — design003 ran comfortably).
